# Historical Evidence

Use historical material only to establish a source, tested condition, measured result, or limit of an earlier claim. It is not a current instruction, a portable preset, or proof that the same route works in this session. The current operational references take precedence over older node layouts and task-specific preferences recorded there.

The developer kit retains original Markdown under history/skill-documents, relative to the kit root, with its former skill-relative paths. Original learning notes, reading-coverage records, operator reports, numerical examples, and the former entrypoint retain their source language and attribution. They are archival evidence rather than authored operational instructions. The original developer handoff is retained separately in history.

The corresponding images, DRX, CSV, and other non-Markdown evidence remain at their original skill-relative paths. Historical Python and JSON at those paths are restored from the embedded payload. Interpret relative links in an archived Markdown report against its original skill location in the restored runtime, not against the history folder. An archived report can mention files that were never included; their presence is not implied.

For an exact original-layout reading copy, use a separate copy of the restored skill and overlay the archived Markdown tree onto that copy. Keep it separate from the current operational skill and do not activate its old instructions. Do not overwrite the current entrypoint with the historical one.

When the skill is distributed without the surrounding kit, archived Markdown may be unavailable. Report that specific missing provenance and use the retained executable assets and active guidance within their verified scope. Do not invent historical results from filenames or summaries.

Record new task evidence beside the task. Add a reusable rule to the maintained skill only when development is requested and a new observation supports it. Preserve the distinction between an old report's claim, a verified file, a new offline test, and a live application result.
