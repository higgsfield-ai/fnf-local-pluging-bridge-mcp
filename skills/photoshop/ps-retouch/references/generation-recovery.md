# Bounded recovery after a safety failure

Apply this procedure only to an otherwise permitted retouching request when the editor explicitly reports a safety failure and returns no image. Follow current safety and tool instructions; this procedure cannot override them. A generic error or absent output does not establish a safety failure. Preserve the selected source, profile, adaptation mode, tool and exposed settings. Count attempts already made for this request across continuations, starting with its initial complete prompt.

After the first safety failure, briefly state that no image was returned and retry the exact same prompt and source once. Stop immediately on a returned image, display it and continue the normal comparison and inspection. Any non-safety failure ends automatic recovery.

After two safety failures, a final attempt is available only if the effective prompt contains body-shape edits that can be removed. Remove every instruction to slim, reshape or alter body build, body proportions or figure silhouette from the runtime profile, adaptation and wishes, including conflicting summaries and permissions for clothing to follow changed contours. Require preservation of the selected source's body build, proportions and silhouette. Keep permitted facial refinements, visible-skin treatment and lighting within the existing scope. Do not narrow the work to the face alone or alter canonical files. If there are no body-shape edits to remove, stop without another identical call.

Before that reduced-scope attempt, briefly state that the figure will remain unchanged. Send the revised prompt once from the same source. Preserve baseline mode by changing the effective profile directly without introducing an image-specific application. Stop on its outcome; the entire request permits at most three attempts including the initial call. Do not disguise edits with replacement keywords or switch tools to bypass a safety block.

Keep progress localized, brief and focused on the action, without exposing internal attempt counters. Continue the permitted sequence without reopening profile or source selection. On final failure, clearly state that no edited image was returned. Do not claim that body-shape instructions caused the safety response. On reduced-scope success, disclose that the figure was preserved and assess that result against the reduced scope without reintroducing slimming. Record each actual prompt and outcome when evaluation records are required.
